# AI-native depth over general design-tool breadth

After benchmarking against [Uwarp](https://www.uwarp.design/), we decided to expand our resource layer only within the AI-native UI boundary rather than competing with Uwarp's broad catalog of general design tools (image converters, color pickers, audio tools, design games, etc.).

The site remains an **AI interface toolkit and resource hub**: copy-paste components, first-party browser tools, a curated directory of AI UI resources, and evidence-driven case studies/guides. We will not chase Uwarp's volume by adding generic utilities unrelated to AI product interfaces.

This keeps every new asset and tool connected to our existing component ecosystem and pattern library, preserves a differentiated SEO position, and avoids a race we cannot win against a 500+ tool directory.
